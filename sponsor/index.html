What is Waskill?

Waskill is a free tool for Claude beginners. You paste the prompt you're about to send to Claude, and Waskill suggests the Claude Agent Skills (SKILL.md) that fit the task, picked from popular open-source GitHub repos. Matching runs in the browser, and prompts aren't sent anywhere.

Audience

Waskill is just launching. Its visitors are people who use Claude and Claude Code and want better results from it: builders, marketers, analysts and other professionals picking skills for real tasks.

What sponsors get

  • A sponsor card under the results. One quiet card, clearly labeled “Sponsored”, shown after the top 3 skill suggestions (never above them). It can show for every prompt, or only for prompts that match your keywords.
  • A mention in the Waskill newsletter, the short weekly email about new and trending Claude skills.

One sponsor at a time, and only tools that are useful to Claude users.
